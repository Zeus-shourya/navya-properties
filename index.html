/ 03 — How it goes

From “interesting”
to “it’s registered.”

Four steps, all of them run by our own people. No sub-brokers, no handoffs, and the same faces from the first call to the mutation entry.

01

Discover

Tell us the budget, the purpose and the timeframe. We shortlist what genuinely fits and say plainly what does not exist at that number.

02

Visit

We drive you out, walk the full boundary, and put you in front of the actual owner. Ask anything you like while you are standing on it.

03

Verify

Jamabandi, mutation chain, encumbrance, acquisition notices and access. If the paper does not hold, we walk away from it with you.

04

Own

Registration at the tehsil, boundary marked with the seller present, and the mutation followed until the entry reads your name.

/ Operating principle

We still refuse listings we have not personally walked.

It is the slowest way to build an inventory. A portal can carry two thousand properties because nobody has seen any of them; we carry a fraction of that because someone from this office has stood on each one, pulled the record, and traced the transfers back through the family.

When a title will not hold up, we tell the owner first and the buyer second, and the property never reaches this website. That has cost us deals. It is also the only reason anyone sends us their brother-in-law.
