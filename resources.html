/ 02 — Land units

One bigha is
not one bigha.

It is the single most common misunderstanding on a site visit. The bigha varies by state, so this converter uses the pucca bigha of Haryana and western Uttar Pradesh — 3,025 square yards. If the seller means a Rajasthan or Bihar bigha, the number on the ground changes and you should say so out loud before agreeing a rate.

Haryana / west-UP conventions: 1 acre = 4,840 sq yd = 8 kanal = 160 marla. 1 killa is one acre.
